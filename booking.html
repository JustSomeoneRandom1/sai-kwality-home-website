YOUR HOME IN WEST THURROCK

A WELCOMING PLACE TO STAY

Space to relax,
room to feel at home.

Sai Kwality Home is a serviced stay in West Thurrock, Grays, offering a comfortable base for work trips, contractor visits, shopping breaks and family stays.

The property brings together a living area, bedrooms, kitchen, dining space, bathroom and garden. Whether you are staying for a few nights or arranging a longer visit, you can settle in and enjoy space to unwind after a busy day.

Find us: 2A First Avenue, West Thurrock, Grays, Essex, RM20 3JR
